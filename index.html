Hire the café

Take over the whole space on Victoria Road for a birthday, a book launch, a team breakfast, or a small celebration — concrete counter, warm wood, the orange wall, and our people looking after you. Seats up to 28 guests.

  • Flat fee — R2,000. The space is yours. Waffles and coffee are sold as normal, and your guests order and pay individually at the counter.
  • Flat fee + R80 per guest. Same R2,000 flat rate, plus R80 per guest — and we cover The Original waffle or waffle minis and coffee for everyone, so it's a free event for your guests.
  • Flat fee + R120 per guest (gourmet). Same R2,000 flat rate, plus R120 per guest — everyone gets a gourmet flavour waffle or waffle minis (Biscoff Dream, Dark & Oreo, Belgian Chocolate) and coffee included.

Ready to hire the café?

Send your date, guest count, and which pricing option you'd like — we'll get back to you to confirm availability. Need us to come to you instead? That's what the cart below is for.

Email cafe@quiverfoundation.org

Book the Cart

We bring barista-made coffee and hot Belgian Liège waffles to your birthday, wedding, market or company day — anywhere within 30 km of Woodstock, Cape Town. Half day is 4 hours of service, full day is 8, cashless on the day, all prices include VAT.
